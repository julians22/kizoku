# Privacy Policy

KIZOKU values your privacy and is committed to safeguarding your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information, as well as your rights under applicable Personal Data Protection laws.

## Collection of Personal Data

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including when you:

- Register for an account
- Provide your phone number
- Provide your email address
- Log in via Facebook or Google

## Types of Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

- **Personal details:** such as name, surname, gender, date of birth
- **Contact information:** including address, phone number, and email
- **Account information:** such as username, password, and transaction history
- **Financial and transaction data:** such as purchase history, payment details, and bank account information
- **Technical data:** including IP address, cookie identifiers, and activity logs
- **Other data:** such as photos, videos, or any information considered personal data under applicable laws

## Children's Privacy

If you are under 20 years of age or legally restricted, we may collect and process your personal data only with consent from a parent or legal guardian, or as permitted by law. If we discover that we have collected a child's data without proper consent, we will take steps to delete it.

## Use of Personal Data

We use your personal data to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Deliver products and services
- Improve our offerings and user experience
- Manage internal operations
- Conduct marketing and promotional activities
- Provide customer support and after-sales services
- Collect feedback
- Process payments
- Enforce our Terms and Conditions
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

## Disclosure of Personal Data

We may share your personal data in the following cases:

- **Within our organization:** to support and improve our services
- **Service providers:** such as payment processors and developers (subject to their own privacy policies)
- **Business partners:** to coordinate services and provide relevant product information

## Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Once no longer needed, your data will be deleted, destroyed, or anonymized.

## Your Rights

Under applicable laws, you have the right to:

- Withdraw your consent at any time
- Access your personal data and request copies
- Request data portability in a machine-readable format
- Object to the processing of your data
- Request deletion, destruction, or anonymization
- Request restriction of processing
- Correct or update inaccurate data
- File a complaint with relevant authorities

To exercise your rights, please contact us through e-mail: **karuizawaindonesia@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days. If your request is denied, we will explain the reason via appropriate communication channels.

We may use your data to personalize content, advertisements, and marketing campaigns through tools such as Google, Facebook, and tracking technologies.

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, personalize content, and analyze website usage. You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. These measures include administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

## Data Breach Notification
