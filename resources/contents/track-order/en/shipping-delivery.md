# Shipping & Delivery

## What shipping carriers do you use to deliver orders?

We offer several courier options for delivery, including:

1. JNE
2. J&T Express
3. SiCepat
4. TIKI

Available shipping options may vary depending on your location.

## Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only offer domestic shipping within Indonesia. We hope to offer international shipping in the future. Thank you for your understanding!

## How can I track my package once it has been shipped?

You can track your package through the selected courier's official website by entering your receipt number.

## How long does it take to process and ship an order?

We aim to process and ship orders as quickly as possible. Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days and shipped within 3–5 business days. Delivery times may vary depending on your location and the shipping option selected at checkout.

## Can I change my shipping address or shipping method after placing an order?

If you need to change your shipping address or shipping method after placing an order, please contact us via WhatsApp: Karuizawa Shirt (+62 852-1909-8497).

Please provide your order number or receipt, along with the updated shipping details or the changes you would like to request.

Please note that changes may not be possible once your order has been processed or shipped.
