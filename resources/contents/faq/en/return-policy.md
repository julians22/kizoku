## Return Policy

### What is your product return policy?

1. Products must be in perfect condition, unopened, in their original packaging, with the seal fully intact.
2. Returns or exchanges cannot be made for incorrect purchases made by the customer, including errors in selecting the product, model, or scent, as well as products purchased from other distributors.
3. Product returns cannot be made based on personal preference, including dissatisfaction with the product or its scent, as the character and intensity of a fragrance may differ according to individual taste.

### How do I submit a complaint about a product?

If you experience an issue with your order, make sure the entire unboxing process is recorded on video from the start. The video is required to process any complaint or warranty claim.

### How do I submit a product return?

**In-Store Purchases**

Please bring the product along with the original purchase receipt to the store where the purchase was made. Our store staff will assist with the return or exchange process. Requests must be submitted within 7 days of the purchase date.

**Website Orders**

Please contact us via Karuizawa Shirt WhatsApp (+62 852-1909-8497). Kindly include your purchase receipt or order number when submitting a return or exchange request.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility. All return requests must be submitted within 7 days of receiving the order.
