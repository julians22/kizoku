## Shipping

### When will my order arrive?

Delivery time depends on your location. The estimated delivery time will be provided during checkout.

### Can I change the shipping method after placing an order?

Shipping methods generally cannot be changed once the order has been processed. If you need assistance, please contact our customer service as soon as possible.

### My order status shows it has been delivered, but I haven't received it. What should I do?

Please first check with people around your delivery address, such as family members, neighbors, or the building receptionist. If the package is still not found, contact our customer service for further assistance.

### What if I need very fast delivery?

For urgent delivery needs, you can visit our store and make a purchase directly. Alternatively, you can contact our store staff via WhatsApp to place an online order. The store team can then arrange same-day delivery using a courier service.
