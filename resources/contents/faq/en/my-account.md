## My Account

### Do I need to register to place an order?

No, you do not need to register to place an order. However, by creating an account, you can save your information to make future purchases easier and receive promotional emails. You can manage promotional email preferences at any time through the newsletter settings.

### What are the benefits of creating an account?

Yes. Customers who create an account receive **10% off** their **first purchase**.

### How can I change my password or personal information?

You can update your password and personal information at any time through the **My Account** section after logging in to your account.
